BEFORE WE GET STARTED

Good questions.
Honest answers.

How much does a website cost?+

It depends on the pages, content, and features you need. Tell us a little about your business and we’ll put together a clear scope and price before you decide to go ahead.

Do I need my content ready?+

No. Start with what you have: a description of your business, services or menu, contact details, and any photos or branding. We’ll discuss what’s missing and agree on the help you need.

What if I already have a website?+

Send us the link and tell us what isn’t working for you. We can discuss a refresh or a new site once we understand your current setup and what you want to change.

How long will it take?+

Timing depends on the scope, how much content is ready, and the feedback needed. We’ll agree on a realistic schedule with you before starting.

Can you help after launch?+

Yes. We can discuss optional care for content changes and upkeep. Any ongoing work, costs, and response expectations will be agreed separately so you know what’s included.

Do we have to meet in person?+

No. We can work together remotely by email or phone. If you’re near Parkville and prefer a local conversation, let us know and we can discuss meeting.
